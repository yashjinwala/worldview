# Worldview & vision

A personal worldview about **agency in the age of abundant intelligence**. Descriptive
first (what's true and coming), then normative (what's worth doing). Written to be
revised.

## The spine

Intelligence is becoming abundant and nearly free. So the scarce, valuable thing is no
longer *having* intelligence — it's **direction**: knowing where to point it, and
knowing what's good.

## What follows (descriptive)

- **Story vs. reality.** AI's narrative races ahead of its real capability. The reality
  is physically bottlenecked — compute now, energy next — and current tech may never
  reach the long tail of real problems. The story does more work than the truth, and the
  gap persists.
- **Harnessing vs. consuming.** The line between AI amplifying you and AI hollowing you
  out is direction. Steer it and you're a harnesser — you get amplified. Passively
  receive its output and you're a consumer — you get hollowed. Same tool, opposite fates.
- **The divide hardens.** Not smart vs. dumb — harnessers vs. consumers. It won't close
  in five years; it hardens. And it's emotional as much as technical: the frontier is
  thrilled, the majority is scared.
- **Value migrates to the unrepossessable.** Architecture commoditizes — the frontier
  labs "come for everything" general. What survives is owned, distilled, idiosyncratic
  experience they can't repossess: proprietary data, real relationships, trust,
  distribution, and **taste** at the moving frontier (too fast and social to pre-train).
- **"They come for everything" = "intelligence becomes free."** Same coin. The labs
  eating the general layer is the very force that concentrates all remaining value into
  the specific, owned, directed layer.

## The axis underneath all of it: agency

Every belief above is really about one thing — **who gets to author their life when
intelligence is free, and who gets authored by it.** AI is just what forces the question.

## What's worth doing (normative / the vision)

Don't accept the hardened divide. Bring the consumers along.

The majority is deathly scared, and every version of that fear — losing your job, being
made stupid, being fooled, being left behind — is the same fear: **loss of control.**
Fear makes people freeze and refuse, which makes them fall behind, which hardens the
divide and deepens the fear. Left alone, that loop ends in mass hollowing or political
backlash.

The antidote is felt agency:

> **Give every ordinary person their first real, felt hit of directing AI to create
> value _they_ control. That moment of agency turns a scared consumer into an author —
> and it's the only thing that keeps the future from fracturing into the empowered and
> the harnessed.**

## The lens (for finding what to build)

- A **problem worth solving** = anything that keeps ordinary people stuck on the scared,
  harnessed side of the divide; anything that blocks the first felt hit of agency.
- A **good solution** = anything that hands an ordinary person that first felt hit of
  _directed, controlled_ value. They steer; their fingerprints are on the output.
- **Fails the lens:** doing it _for_ them (crutch/consumer), serving the already-fluent
  frontier, or building in the general layer the labs will repossess.
