# Ideas & direction log

A living scratchpad for where the product is pointed — what we're keeping, what we
killed, and the candidate problems still on the table. Not a spec. The spec is
[`PRD.md`](PRD.md) / [`TDD.md`](TDD.md); this is the layer above it where the
problem itself is still being chosen.

---

## What survives no matter what (the asset)

The thing worth keeping is the **engine**, independent of the problem it serves:

- It makes you **reason yourself** instead of handing you the answer — a loop only
  closes when *you* say the insight back in your own words.
- It **reads how you're reasoning** per turn (engagement × proximity to a click) and
  adapts the guide's move.
- It **remembers how *you* think** across sessions — a persistent per-user model. This
  is the moat.
- It can deliver the "click" as an **interactive** when prose won't.
- It is **lean-in by nature** — it demands attention; that's the point, not a flaw.

## What we killed, and why

**Curiosity / "thinking atrophy" for the general off-hours wonderer.** The insight
(we're offloading our thinking to AI and getting worse at it) is real, but aimed at
people who feel it as **guilt** — a vitamin, not a painkiller. A problem people
"don't realize they have" is two sales: first the disease, then the cure. It also
forced a **lean-out** form factor (ambient widgets, voice-on-a-walk) onto a
**lean-in** asset, and the two fought. The conviction drain was that mismatch, not a
bad engine.

**Key correction:** the atrophy insight wasn't wrong — it was pointed at the wrong
population. Point the same insight at people who feel it as **fear** (a dated
deadline where reasoning-it-yourself has consequences) and the vitamin becomes a
painkiller. Same asset, same "won't hand you the answer" mechanic.

## The live reframe: high-stakes reasoning prep

Where ChatGPT is *actively making people worse and they can feel it.* A cohort now
studies *with* ChatGPT: they read the worked solution, it makes sense, they feel
ready — then freeze on the next problem, because recognition isn't recall. The
engine's core rule (the loop doesn't close until *you* produce the reasoning) is a UX
nicety in a curiosity app and a **pedagogical necessity** here. The wedge —
"the prep tool that won't let you fool yourself the way ChatGPT does" — is sharp,
ownable, and 2026-fresh.

Why the asset is purpose-built for it:

| Asset | In reasoning prep |
|---|---|
| Won't hand you the answer | The difference between *feeling* ready and *being* ready — the exact failure mode of ChatGPT-study (testing effect / active recall). |
| Reads how you reason per turn | Diagnoses *why* you missed — conceptual hole vs. careless vs. wrong frame — not just that you did. Question banks can't. |
| Persistent model of how you think | Models *your* weak spots and failure patterns over weeks. What a $200/hr tutor does. The moat. |
| The Examiner endpoint (already designed) | Becomes the **mock-under-questioning**: defend your reasoning while it pushes back. Central, not vestigial. |

What dies quietly with this reframe: **constraint #2 ("value can't require opening
the app") evaporates** — a deadline-driven user opens the app out of fear. Lean-in,
which is what the asset actually is. Spaced-repetition reminders are still fair game,
but that's an honest "your review is due" nudge, not forced-ambient delivery.

Honest caveat: test prep is crowded — but the crowd is *content* (question banks,
videos, Anki). None is an adaptive Socratic coach that refuses to cheat for you and
models your gaps.

## Candidate niches (selector: reasoning-dominant + can you reach 10 real people?)

- **Case interviews (consulting).** ⭐ The format is *literally* reason-out-loud-and-
  defend-under-challenge — the best Examiner fit. Funded, anxious, deadline-driven,
  already paying humans $100–300/hr for mock partners.
- **System-design / technical interviews (SWE).** Huge, funded, reasoning-not-recall;
  the "ChatGPT made me worse" pain is most acute here.
- **LSAT logical reasoning / logic games.** Pure reasoning, zero memorization, the
  most ChatGPT-resistant; anxious pre-law market that pays.

Memorization-heavy exams (MCAT/USMLE-style) dilute the asset and are lower priority.

## Open questions

- **Which niche, by reachability?** The selector now is which population we can put in
  front of ten real users next week — that's the test that earns conviction back.
- Is the differentiation ("won't cheat for you + models your gaps") legible to a buyer
  *before* they've felt it, or does it only land after a session?
- Does the persistent per-user model produce a visibly better experience by week 3
  than a stateless tutor? (The moat has to be demonstrable, not asserted.)

# Session 2 — beyond Worldview: solving tomorrow's problems

Reframe: stop solving yesterday's problems. Derive problem spaces from where the
world is heading, not from what has search volume today (tomorrow's problems don't
have search volume yet). Scope expanded beyond Worldview, beyond Gen Z.

## The master thesis

**Intelligence is becoming abundant and nearly free; judgment is becoming the scarce
bottleneck — and nobody is building to develop it.** By ~2030 the binding question
isn't "can we produce the answer" (machines will, cheaply) but "can a human tell
whether it's good, and own it?" Meanwhile every institution that used to *manufacture*
judgment — apprenticeship, the bottom rung, school, struggling with a hard problem
yourself — is being automated away at the same moment. We're building tools that make
people more productive and less capable, simultaneously.

Note: the **engine** Worldview already has (makes you reason, won't hand you the
answer, models how you think) is a *judgment-development engine*. It was forward-looking
all along; only the problem framings (curiosity, test prep) were backward-looking.

## Three candidate futures

1. **Judgment crisis** (likely, 3–7 yrs) — AI eats entry-level knowledge work, the
   rungs where judgment was built; "missing middle" competence crisis. *Uses the engine.
   High conviction, early.*
2. **Caregiving cliff** (near-certain, demographic) — boomers age into deep care through
   the 2030s; care-worker shortage; $80T+ wealth transfer; sandwich gen squeezed. A
   "caregiving OS." *Fresh build, surest bet, real payer. Orthogonal to the AI thesis.*
3. **Authenticity collapse** (likely, 2–5 yrs) — generative content flips the default to
   "fake until proven real." *Most concrete, but biometric verification is already
   crowded/funded; the open layer is peer-to-peer "proof of real" + the "will I be
   believed?" deniability harm.*

## The frontier-research convergence (strongest signal of the session)

Three research threads — AI-mediated dating/friendship, authenticity/trust collapse,
AI-companion fallout — collapsed into ONE thesis:

> **Gen Z is quietly outsourcing its hardest human moments to AI (the rejection, the
> apology, the boundary, the confession, the comfort) — and the wound isn't the
> outsourcing, it's the erosion of trust in their own unaided voice, in secret, with
> shame. The AI fills the gap left by inaccessible humans, then becomes the reason
> humans stay inaccessible.**

This is the atrophy insight with teeth: a population that feels it as *fear/shame/grief*
(not the diffuse guilt that made it a vitamin for general knowledge workers), it's the
leading edge of the master thesis, it subsumes the judgment future and touches
authenticity, and it's the cohort we understand.

Solid evidence (separated from vendor fluff):
- *The Conversation*, n>1,300: secret AI use in personal messages is undetectable and
  rewarded; disclosed use is punished as "lazy/insincere." The perverse incentive is the
  engine of trust erosion. (peer-reviewed)
- Named voices: *"Chat was my backbone"*; the rejection text read as *"99% AI"*; expert:
  *"it may instill the belief that your own words and instincts are never good enough."*
- Common Sense Media: 72% of teens have used AI companions, ~33% for emotional support;
  Character.AI under-18 ban produced real teen grief.

Why the engine is the rare right tool: the paradox is "software broke this; why would
software fix it?" Every product in the space (rizz apps, companions) is a **crutch** that
deepens the wound. The only defensible design is the **anti-crutch** — make you *able to
do the hard human thing yourself*, then get out of the way. That is the engine's founding
rule verbatim.

Honest stress-test:
- **Magnitude is soft** — phenomenon well-evidenced, size leans on dating/security-vendor
  surveys; Reddit was unreachable; richest communities skew older than 17–27.
- **"Software can't fix what software broke"** is unresolved — if the cure is offline, the
  product can at best be a coach that pushes you off itself.
- **Anti-crutch fights its own retention** — but here that's a *feature*: the user wants to
  graduate off their dependency. Reframes monetization from "keep them hooked" to "the
  thing they're proud to outgrow."

## Niche-tribe candidates (a different SHAPE of bet — narrow vertical tools, not the big thesis)

Banked from research; sharpest open gaps, roughly ranked:
1. **Apprenticeship hour-logging as legal evidence** (electrician/cosmetology) —
   ~8,000 hrs across categories, each needing a journeyman *sign-off as proof*; a
   spiteful boss can refuse to sign and hold a career hostage. White-collar time-trackers
   ignore the third-party-attestation requirement. Immutable, timestamped, signed trail.
2. **First-gen family bureaucracy decoder** — children of immigrants are their family's
   "bureaucracy interface" (brokering from age ~11: aid letters, bills, medical, legal).
   Backed by a peer-reviewed study with quotes; emotional + practical; nobody tools "I am
   my family's interface to institutions." Not a translator — a decoder + decision-tracker.
3. **RSD-safe task-initiation / communication** for neurodivergent Gen Z — the blocker is
   rejection-sensitive dysphoria at the point of *sending a message*, not focus. Distinct
   from the crowded body-doubling/timer/habit space.
4. **Creator disclosure-compliance tracking** + **DM-native brand-deal→payment pipeline** —
   the unglamorous gaps left after avoiding the crowded link-in-bio/scheduling/repurposing.
5. **Cosplay multi-build component tracker** — community building its own spreadsheets = the
   unmet-need signal.

**Crowded — avoid:** rizz/reply-drafting apps, biometric proof-of-humanity, link-in-bio,
scheduling, cross-platform repurposing, generic body-doubling and ADHD timer apps,
AI-DM-generation tools.

## Update — the apprenticeship/credential-collapse thread (hardens future #1)

Strong evidence the "judgment crisis" future is already underway:
- **Gallup** (n=1,572 Gen Z, Feb–Mar 2026, ±3.6): 8 in 10 say AI will make it *harder to
  learn*; 42% say it's harmful to critical thinking; 38% say it undermines independent
  idea generation. Quantified "what's the point of getting good at something" anxiety.
- **Stanford "Canaries"** (ADP payroll, 3.5–5M workers): entry-level hiring in AI-exposed
  jobs down ~13%; devs 22–25 down ~20% from 2022 peak while 30+ grew. (Authors: causal
  evidence "suggestive"; a Denmark study found no effect — don't overclaim AI causation.)
- **SignalFire**: new grads now 7% of Big Tech hires (−50% vs 2019); "employers want proof,
  not potential"; junior roles posted but filled with senior ICs.
- **The broken bargain** (Stack Overflow / Addy Osmani): juniors traded grunt work for
  mentorship; AI does the grunt work now, so firms lost the incentive to fund the ramp —
  "an ecosystem that stops training its replacements." Only ad-hoc fixes exist (Stripe's
  late-2025 new-grad program: review AI code + pair with seniors instead of shipping).
- **Signal degradation** (Wang, arXiv 2511.00068): AI output no longer reliably signals
  ability because low-ability agents launder effort through it → the market wants a new,
  harder-to-fake competence signal and doesn't have one.

Concrete OPEN gaps (the wedge for future #1):
1. **A trustworthy, AI-resistant proof-of-competence signal** to replace the degree —
   verifiable that the work wasn't AI-laundered. (Resume/ATS/portfolio cosmetics are
   saturated; the *verification/trust* layer is the real gap.)
2. **An AI-era apprenticeship substitute** — a structured loop that rebuilds judgment now
   that the grunt-work curriculum is automated. Nothing scaled fills this. *This is the
   judgment-development engine's home turf.*

Crowded — avoid: job-search/resume/ATS tools, micro-credential marketplaces, "future of
work" thought-leadership.

## The open decision

Bet on the **convergence** (social/relational capability + self-trust; uses the engine;
forward-looking; our cohort) — or the **caregiving cliff** (demographic certainty, fresh
build) — or pick one of the **niche-tribe** vertical tools (concrete, smaller, more
clearly a painkiller). Recommendation leans convergence; caregiving is the safe orthogonal
alternative. Next step on whichever: pressure-test into a concrete wedge — the specific
first user and the specific first hard-moment we help them do *themselves*.
